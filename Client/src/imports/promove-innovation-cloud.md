Build ProMove Innovation Cloud (PIC)
Create a scalable SaaS platform called ProMove Innovation Cloud (PIC).
The platform will function as the world’s first operating system for student innovators, enabling young people (school and college students) to transform real-world problems into products, patents, and startups.
The platform must combine the capabilities of:
innovation labs
startup incubators
product development workspaces
intellectual property support systems
startup launch infrastructure
All integrated into a single innovation ecosystem.
The system must support millions of users globally, including students, schools, colleges, mentors, companies, and investors.
🌍 PLATFORM CORE PHILOSOPHY
The platform should operate on a Problem → Product → Patent → Startup pipeline.
Users begin by identifying problems, develop ideas, build prototypes, protect intellectual property, and launch startups.
The platform must act as a global innovation infrastructure layer for education systems.
